About

Building trustworthy, reproducible healthcare intelligence

My work sits at the intersection of healthcare data engineering, health informatics, artificial intelligence, and data governance. I am especially interested in methods that make healthcare data and AI systems more trustworthy, explainable, reproducible, and useful for clinical, operational, analytical, and decision-support applications.

Current public work includes synthetic healthcare benchmark datasets, explainable healthcare analytics, healthcare data-quality research, clinical NLP, and reproducible studies based on public healthcare data.

Research Areas

Core research themes

Healthcare Data Quality

Explainable and evidence-based approaches for assessing the fitness, provenance, consistency, and trustworthiness of healthcare data.

Healthcare AI & Explainability

Machine-learning and AI methods designed for interpretable, auditable, and decision-support use in healthcare environments.

Benchmark Datasets

Reproducible synthetic and public-data benchmarks for evaluating healthcare AI, clinical NLP, classification, extraction, and related methods.

CMS & Health-Plan Analytics

Longitudinal analysis of Medicare, utilization, enrollment, risk, and health-plan decision variables using public healthcare data.

Clinical NLP

Evaluation of summarization, diagnosis prediction, procedure prediction, visit classification, and structured extraction from synthetic clinical notes.

Reproducible Healthcare Research

Research artifacts designed with documented data provenance, validation, versioning, code, and reproducibility in mind.
