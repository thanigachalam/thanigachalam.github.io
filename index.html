About

Building trustworthy, reproducible healthcare intelligence

My work sits at the intersection of healthcare data engineering, health informatics, artificial intelligence, and data governance. I am especially interested in systems that make healthcare data more trustworthy, explainable, reproducible, and useful for clinical, operational, analytical, and decision-support applications.

Current work includes open healthcare data-quality frameworks, synthetic benchmark datasets, explainable healthcare analytics, and human-governed AI workflow architectures.

Research Areas

Core research themes

Healthcare Data Quality

Explainable, evidence-based methods for assessing the fitness, provenance, trustworthiness, and governance of healthcare data.

Healthcare AI & Explainability

Machine-learning and AI systems designed for interpretable, auditable, decision-support use in healthcare and health-plan environments.

Benchmark Datasets

Reproducible synthetic and public-data benchmarks for evaluating healthcare AI, clinical NLP, administrative workflows, and data-quality methods.

CMS & Health-Plan Analytics

Longitudinal analysis of Medicare, utilization, enrollment, risk, and health-plan decision variables using public healthcare data.

Human-Governed AI Workflows

Architectures where AI can observe, reason, recommend, and learn while execution remains governed by explicit approval and accountability controls.

Local & Privacy-Preserving AI

Healthcare AI designs that can operate in local, restricted, or disconnected environments where sensitive data should not leave organizational boundaries.

Selected programs and projects

Framework

OpenHDQ — Open Healthcare Data Quality

An open, implementation-neutral framework for assessing, explaining, evidencing, and governing the fitness and trustworthiness of healthcare data for defined use cases.

Data quality • Trust • Governance • Local deployment
AI Workflow Research

HAWA — Human-Governed Autonomous Workflow Intelligence

A healthcare workflow research program built around the principle Autonomous Intelligence, Controlled Execution, combining standards, datasets, models, agents, approvals, verification, and learning.

Agents • Approval • Accountability • Workflow intelligence

A synthetic clinical-notes benchmark designed for tasks such as summarization, diagnosis prediction, procedure prediction, visit classification, and structured extraction.
